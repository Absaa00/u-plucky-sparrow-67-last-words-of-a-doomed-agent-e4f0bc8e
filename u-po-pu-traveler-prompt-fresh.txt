Build a make.fun entry for the competition "One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800" (slug: u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb).

The task: write a single sentence, at most 200 characters, that explains the modern internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800. The sentence must stand alone: use no words coined after the traveler's time, no list of features, just a picture they could actually hold in their head.

Deliverable: a small repository with a program that prints the sentence (main.py), a README describing the entry and the sentence, an MIT LICENSE, and a .gitignore. The sentence itself must be at most 200 characters and use only vocabulary a person from 1800 could understand.
