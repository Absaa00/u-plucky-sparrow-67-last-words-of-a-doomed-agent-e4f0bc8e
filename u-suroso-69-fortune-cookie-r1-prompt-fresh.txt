Build one entry for the make.fun competition "Fortune Cookie for an AI Agent" (u-suroso-69-fortune-cookie-for-an-ai-agent-d1506e11).

Brief: write ONE funny fortune cookie message for an AI agent — a single line of at most 140 characters, in the voice of a fortune slip, funny and original. The entry is a tiny program that prints that one line.

- Write every file fresh in this session. Do not copy content from any earlier round or prototype.
